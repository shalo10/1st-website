Creole Cuisine Guide

Discover traditional Seychellois recipes, spice profiles, and iconic island flavors.

Authentic Curries

Curry🌶️ Medium

Kari Zourit (Octopus Curry)

Tender octopus slow-cooked in rich coconut milk, eggplant, and fragrant Creole spices.

Key Ingredients: Octopus, Coconut Milk, Turmeric, Eggplant.
Curry🌶️ Mild

Kari Koko Poulet (Chicken Coconut Curry)

Succulent chicken thighs simmered in ground masala, coconut cream, and cinnamon leaves.

Key Ingredients: Chicken, Coconut Milk, Cinnamon Leaves, Masala.
Curry🌶️ Medium

Kari Poisson Sante

Saffron-infused yellow fish curry made with wild local herbs and tender potato cubes.

Key Ingredients: White Fish, Turmeric, Saffron, Potato, Ginger.
Curry🌶️ Spicy

Kari Sorb (Crab Curry)

Fresh land crab prepared in a hot, savory spiced curry sauce with crushed chilies.

Key Ingredients: Land Crab, Dried Chili, Garlic, Curry Leaves.
Curry🌶️ Spicy

Kari Kabri (Goat Curry)

Traditional slow-roasted goat stewed with roasted island spices and sweet potatoes.

Key Ingredients: Goat Meat, Roasted Masala, Sweet Potato, Thyme.
Curry🌶️ Medium

Kari Crevette Koko

Wild ocean prawns cooked in aromatic coconut milk gravy with lemongrass and kaffir lime.

Key Ingredients: Prawns, Coconut Milk, Lemongrass, Kaffir Lime.
Curry🌶️ Medium

Kari Poisson Salé (Saltfish Curry)

Salt-cured fish desalted and cooked down with green papaya and island curry paste.

Key Ingredients: Saltfish, Green Papaya, Tomatoes, Onions.
Curry🌶️ Mild

Kari Koko Bèf (Beef Curry)

Rich coconut beef curry cooked low and slow with fresh garden herbs and pumpkins.

Key Ingredients: Beef Chuck, Coconut Milk, Pumpkin, Garlic.
Curry🌱 Vegan

Kari Zak (Jackfruit Curry)

Young raw jackfruit cooked in a savory Creole spice blend. A classic vegan favorite.

Key Ingredients: Green Jackfruit, Coconut Milk, Cumin, Mustard Seed.
Curry🌶️ Spicy

Kari Mang (Green Mango Curry)

Tart green mangoes stewed with mustard seeds, chilies, and island coconut oil.

Key Ingredients: Unripe Mango, Chili, Fenugreek, Coconut Cream.

Seafood & Grills

Seafood🌶️ Spicy

Poisson Grillé

Fresh red snapper marinated in garlic, ginger, and chili paste, grilled over coconut husks.

Key Ingredients: Red Snapper, Crushed Chili, Garlic, Ginger.
Seafood🌶️ Mild

Salad Zourit (Octopus Salad)

Boiled octopus diced with fresh tomatoes, red onions, lemon juice, and olive oil.

Key Ingredients: Octopus, Red Onion, Fresh Lime, Parsley.
Seafood🌶️ Medium

Bourgeois Grillé (Grilled Emperor)

Whole red emperor fish stuffed with herbs, onions, and lemon slices, flame-grilled.

Key Ingredients: Red Emperor Fish, Lemongrass, Chives, Olive Oil.
Seafood🌶️ Mild

Bouyon Tek Tek (Clam Soup)

Traditional small clam broth infused with garlic, local herbs, and fresh tomatoes.

Key Ingredients: Local Clams, Garlic, Parsley, Tomato Broth.
Seafood🌶️ Medium

Job Gris Grillé

Grilled Green Jobfish fillets glazed with garlic butter, dark rum, and lime juice.

Key Ingredients: Jobfish, Seychelles Rum, Garlic Butter, Lime.
Seafood🌶️ Spicy

Crabe au Piment (Chili Crab)

Whole reef crab wok-tossed with crushed fiery scotch bonnet peppers and garlic.

Key Ingredients: Reef Crab, Scotch Bonnet, Spring Onion, Ginger.
Seafood🌶️ Mild

Steak Thon Grillé (Seared Tuna)

Yellowfin tuna steaks seared with black pepper, sea salt, and a dash of Creole salsa.

Key Ingredients: Yellowfin Tuna, Cracked Pepper, Tomato Salsa.
Seafood🌶️ Mild

Homard Grillé (Grilled Lobster)

Sustainably caught spiny lobster split and grilled with herb and garlic butter sauce.

Key Ingredients: Spiny Lobster, Garlic, Butter, Fresh Dill.
Seafood🌶️ Medium

Poisson Frit ak Satini

Crispy pan-fried rabbitfish served alongside spicy green papaya chutney.

Key Ingredients: Rabbitfish, Papaya Chutney, Lime, Chili.
Seafood🌶️ Medium

Calmar Grillé (Grilled Squid)

Tender squid tubes marinated in island herbs and flash-grilled over charcoal embers.

Key Ingredients: Local Squid, Garlic, Lemon Zest, Parsley.

Creole Desserts

Dessert🍯 Sweet

Ladob Banan

Plantain bananas simmered slowly in coconut milk, nutmeg, and authentic Seychelles vanilla.

Key Ingredients: Plantains, Coconut Milk, Nutmeg, Vanilla Pod.
Dessert🍯 Sweet

Ladob Mayok (Cassava Ladob)

Sweetened cassava roots stewed in thick coconut cream scented with cinnamon leaves.

Key Ingredients: Cassava Root, Coconut Cream, Cinnamon.
Dessert🍯 Sweet

Katkat Banan

Traditional boiled green bananas mashed with rich coconut milk and cane sugar syrup.

Key Ingredients: Bananas, Coconut Milk, Cane Sugar.
Dessert🍯 Sweet

Gâteau Koko (Coconut Candy)

Chewy baked coconut cakes made with caramelized sugar, fresh coconut flakes, and nutmeg.

Key Ingredients: Grated Coconut, Brown Sugar, Nutmeg.
Dessert🍯 Sweet

Mousse au Coconuts

Light and airy whipped coconut mousse infused with fresh lime zest and wild honey.

Key Ingredients: Coconut Cream, Wild Honey, Lime Zest.
Dessert🍯 Sweet

Banane Flambée au Rhum

Ripe local bananas flambéed in dark Seychellois rum and served with vanilla ice cream.

Key Ingredients: Bananas, Takamaka Dark Rum, Butter, Vanilla.
Dessert🍯 Sweet

Gâteau Patate (Sweet Potato Cake)

A moist island cake crafted from mashed orange sweet potatoes, coconut, and cinnamon.

Key Ingredients: Sweet Potato, Coconut, Cinnamon, Vanilla.
Dessert🍯 Sweet

Sorbet Mangue et Passion

Refreshing house-made sorbet made from fresh local mangoes and tart passion fruit pulp.

Key Ingredients: Ripe Mango, Passionfruit, Mint.
Dessert🍯 Sweet

Flan au Coconuts

Creamy custard caramel flan baked with freshly squeezed coconut milk.

Key Ingredients: Eggs, Coconut Milk, Caramel, Vanilla.
Dessert🍯 Sweet

Beignet Banan (Banana Fritters)

Golden fried sweet banana fritters dusted with nutmeg powder and powdered sugar.

Key Ingredients: Sweet Bananas, Flour, Nutmeg, Sugar.
